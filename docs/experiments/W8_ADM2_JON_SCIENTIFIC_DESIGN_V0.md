# W8 ADM2 J/O/N common-support design — frozen scientific lead contract
Date: 2026-11-16 · Status: **DESIGN FROZEN BEFORE W8 OUTCOME FITS** · Owner: FCV scientific lead.
Conventions inherit frozen W5/W6/W7 source releases and W7 source-sector native crosswalk. This is a *new descriptive estimand*, not a reanalysis of W7 FE results.

## Question and scope
For donor d in {China, World Bank}, among financing-biennium ADM2 area-periods **comparable within the same country and financing period**, what are the standardized differences in next-biennium ACLED violence-against-civilians incidence between (J) only donor-d candidate-employment/productivity-sector projects, (O) only donor-d other-sector projects, and (N) no China or World Bank source-recorded project **up through t**? J and O are *sector proxies*, not measured paid employment and not reviewer-confirmed labor mechanisms; O is not an employment-free sector; N is unexposed only according to our two observed donors, not all investment globally.

## Freeze: geography / clock / outcomes
- ADM2 only; W5 balanced 6,420 areas × 7 finance biennia (2001–02 through 2013–14), 47 countries; exposure from finance/approval **at t**, not Blair planned/completed implementation dates. Y=indicator of >=1 ACLED VAC event **during next biennium**. No calendar shift, no ADM3, UCDP, DHS or Afrobarometer joins now.
- No population denominator or realized wage bill; area-periods receive statistical weights, **not resident population weights**.
- China and World Bank analyzed separately. A source-qualified cell is (i,t). For each d, J is `d_positive_only` with other donor absent, O is `d_other_only` with other donor absent. Both-donor, within-donor mixed/ambiguous and unknown groups are retained in audit MASTER but **excluded** from each donor's clean triple. Do not relabel either as O or N.
- N requires neither donor projects **at t** plus no China or WB projects in any **earlier represented finance biennium for the same ADM2**. Left-censored prior to 2001 and other financiers remain unobserved. Exclude period-only zero cells with observed prior projects from primary N. O and J retain earlier source exposure, so this is **not** an onset/causal design; report history imbalance.
- Snapshot J and O use **frozen broad v0 sector policy** as primary, and the already-frozen narrow sector policy as a single category-definition sensitivity. If the scenario is narrow, regenerate the corresponding arm panel, never reuse broad arm states.
- Period-only N (no project at t, irrespective of past) is **one prespecified control-purity sensitivity**; it is not the primary and must be labeled weaker control.
- We do NOT use a strict first-recorded J/O exposure as primary: outcome-blind screen left only 354 China J cells and 14 World Bank J cells with co-support; this would address *investment initiation* and is a separate estimand. Do not quietly switch clocks.

## Common strata, weights, and algebra — no trio must be exactly 3 observations
- For each donor separately, define country-period **exact strata** s=(country_iso3,period_id), no outcome-driven matching. Count n_{J,s},n_{O,s},n_{N,s}; keep only s with **all three >0**. Retain all members of each group in the stratum. Not propensity-score or nearest-neighbor matching. This is **coarsened exact matching / standardization at country×period**, not full covariate comparability or formal matched triples.
- Reference distribution: **J-target area-period composition for descriptive standardization (not a causal ATT)**. W_s=n_{J,s}/SUM_s n_{J,s}, where sum includes only retained complete strata. Then MU_g=SUM_s W_s mean(Y|s,g) for g in {J,O,N}. The same W_s applies to ALL three groups. Within group g, each cell in s has analysis weight W_s/n_{g,s}. Print raw counts N_g, distinct ADM2 and countries, effective weighted N_g=1/SUM_{i in group g} w_i^2, country/period weight concentration, and the excluded J share.
- Contrasts B_J=MU_J−MU_N, B_O=MU_O−MU_N and B_JO=MU_J−MU_O=B_J−B_O. Verify |B_JO−(B_J−B_O)|<1e−12 (probability units). Three interpretable differences but two independent parameters. Use **percentage points**, and retain group standardized probabilities as percentages. A negative difference is merely lower modeled recorded VAC area-biennium probability, NOT causal violence prevention.
- Contrast uncertainty: deterministic **cluster bootstrap across countries**, resampling whole countries with replacement (B=1,999, seed=20261116) with all country×period strata and all J/O/N cells intact. Percentile 95% confidence limits are exploratory; G≈10 for broad WB means very weak coverage. Also compute leave-one-country-out min/max for each contrast. No fishing for significance, no post-hoc category redefinition, do not infer true zero from an interval crossing zero. Report bootstrap effective independent country count (1/sum_c W_c^2). p-value optional/omitted rather than unreliable approximation from small-cluster bootstrap.
