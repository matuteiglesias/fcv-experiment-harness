# W7 v0 independent outcome-blind review — qualifications frozen before first fit

Two independent read-only reviewers inspected the W7 v0 policy and prior classification protocol WITHOUT inspecting dependent-variable values or result receipts.

Measurement reviewer: positive denotes a candidate employment/productivity channel, not jobs or wages. Other-sector comparator is heterogeneous and emphatically not a verified non-jobs/placebo arm. Broad/narrow compare sector composition, not alternate verified employment definitions. Multi-tag WB projects spanning opposing sets remain ambiguous. Title signals remain descriptive only.

Econometric reviewer: require exact W5 balanced area×period and unique-key/clock checks, seven-column full rank and contrast-specific residual variance, same-country-period co-support, country-clustered CR1 finite sample, and distinct primary/sensitivity/placebo interpretation. With fewer than 12 countries and 20 country-period strata supporting both contrasted arms (W5 comparator gate), report any estimate only as THIN/DESCRIPTIVE and suppress strong inferential claims, irrespective of nominal p-value. Never hunt favorable coefficients across outcomes/policies. Test FWL vs explicit-dummy OLS on a small synthetic fixture (not 44,940×FE matrix).

These recommendations only strengthen QA and interpretation. The frozen broad/narrow category definitions in config/w7_exploratory_jobs_proxy_v0.json are not changed. The first release still contains no editorially verified employment labels.
